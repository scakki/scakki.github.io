01

2026 · RoLAC Lab

Fault-tolerant humanoid locomotion

I developed SAC controllers and torque limits for a 23-DoF Unitree G1, helping it keep moving under joint-lock faults.

Unitree G1SACMuJoCo
Simulation72%

Walking success under two joint locks

Methods & results +

Challenge. Joint locks change what movements are physically possible. A controller must adapt while managing actuator loads.

Results. Two-lock walking success increased from 0% to 72%. With a hip locked, torque limits reduced peak torque by 26%. A separate crawling policy achieved 92.5% survival with forward progress under three knee/ankle locks, with hips free.

RoLAC Lab · August–September 2026. These are simulation results under distinct fault conditions.

02

2025–present · NASA-funded

Human locomotion & gait discovery

I built a compliant planar biped and a learning pipeline in which walking, running, and skipping emerge from one gait-agnostic reward.

BiomechanicsSACGymnasium
Simulation3 gaits

One reward, without gait labels

Methods & results +

Approach. A 9-DoF model in MuJoCo learns with a single gait-agnostic reward, without reference trajectories.

Evaluation. Frozen controllers achieved 100% fall-free trials across the three gaits, with 0.07–0.26 m/s velocity-tracking RMSE and 0.44–0.84 mechanical cost of transport.

Next. An 18-muscle-actuator model for studying efficient movement in Earth and lunar gravity.

RoLAC Lab · June 2025–present · NASA-funded. Results refer to evaluated simulation trials.

03

2025 · RoLAC Lab

Language-guided robot navigation

I integrated VLM–RL control, LiDAR, RGB-D perception, and A* planning on a physical Go1, then evaluated prompt-injection susceptibility.

VLM / RLROSRealSense
Physical robotGo1

Perception, planning & learned control

Methods & results +

System. Perception and mapping inform high-level goal selection; path planning connects those goals to learned locomotion.

Evaluation. Navigation experiments measured task completion and completion time across randomized physical-robot trials. Security experiments tested redirection without changing VLM weights or training data.

Why it matters. A robot can execute a route successfully while following the wrong high-level instruction. Evaluating autonomy means examining the whole perception-to-action chain.

RoLAC Lab · January–March 2025 · Physical robot experiments.

04

2023–2024 · NSF-funded

MPC vs. reinforcement learning

I built a matched benchmark for PPO and predictive-sampling MPC, comparing efficiency and disturbance recovery under identical conditions.

PPOMPCMuJoCo
Simulation41%

Lower mechanical cost of transport with PPO

Methods & results +

Method. A 12-DoF Go1 at 0.5 m/s, with identical terrain and disturbance conditions for both controllers.

Results. Mechanical cost of transport was 1.768 for PPO versus 2.993 for MPC. Disturbance recovery was 0.25–0.33 s faster for PPO.

Michigan Technological University · August 2023–March 2024 · NSF-funded. Published in 2025; findings apply to the benchmark conditions.
