01 — About

More than just
the research

I grew up in India with two things that never left me — a curiosity for how things move and a pencil in my hand. Before I ever wrote a line of code, I was a freelance artist, making portraits and 3D digital artwork on commission. I served as the Design Head of Yodha — The Warrior Within, a social service organization where I created posters, banners, and visual campaigns.

That creative instinct led me to engineering — first a B.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Visveswaraya Technological University, JSSATE, then an M.S. in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Automation Engineering and doctoral research at Michigan Tech. Today, I'm a Ph.D. candidate in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Binghamton University, working in the RoLAC Lab. My research connects reinforcement learning and control with a question that keeps me curious: how can humans and robots move efficiently across different gravitational environments?

Outside the lab, I'm always chasing something new. Since moving to the U.S., I've picked up skiing, golf, indoor rock climbing, clay sculpting, and I'm a serious 8-ball pool player. Back in India, it was chess, basketball, and cricket. The through-line is simple — I love learning things from scratch, whether it's a new gait controller or a new sport.

01

Fault-Tolerant Humanoid Locomotion

Aug — Sep 2026
Research Assistant · RoLAC Lab, Binghamton University

Developed SAC controllers for a 23-DoF Unitree G1 in MuJoCo, increasing simulated walking success under two joint-lock faults from 0% to 72%. Torque-limiting controls reduced peak torque by 26% (70 to 52 Nm) with a hip joint locked. Crawling policies increased survival with forward progress from 65% to 92.5% under three simultaneous knee/ankle locks with hips free.

02

Human Locomotion & Gait Discovery

Jun 2025 — Present
NASA-funded · Research Assistant, RoLAC Lab

Built a 9-DoF compliant planar biped and an end-to-end SAC pipeline in MuJoCo and Gymnasium. Walking, aerial running, and skipping emerged from one gait-agnostic reward without gait labels, reference trajectories, or prescribed contact schedules. Frozen controllers achieved 100% fall-free trials across the three gaits, with 0.07–0.26 m/s steady-state velocity-tracking RMSE and 0.44–0.84 mechanical cost of transport. Ongoing work develops an 18-muscle-actuator biped for efficiency optimization in Earth and lunar gravity.

03

Autonomous Navigation & Robotic Security

Jan — Mar 2025
Research Assistant · RoLAC Lab

Developed a VLM–RL navigation stack combining LiDAR, RealSense/YOLO perception, A* planning, and learned locomotion on the physical Unitree Go1. Evaluation across 10 randomized trials reported 92% task completion and a 101 s mean completion time. Prompt-injection experiments demonstrated 90% attack success and a 23 s mean redirection time without changing VLM weights or training data.

04

Benchmarking MPC & Reinforcement Learning

Aug 2023 — Mar 2024
NSF-funded · Research Assistant, Michigan Technological University

Developed a standardized MuJoCo benchmark for a 12-DoF Unitree Go1, comparing PPO and predictive-sampling MPC at 0.5 m/s under identical disturbance and terrain conditions. PPO achieved 41% lower mechanical cost of transport (1.768 vs. 2.993) and 0.25–0.33 s faster disturbance recovery. Evaluated robustness under perturbations up to 950 N; published in IEEE Access in 2025.

05

Hardware-in-the-Loop Industrial Assembly

Mar — Apr 2023
Course Project · Michigan Technological University

Integrated an Allen-Bradley PLC with FANUC RoboGuide over EtherNet/IP for a simulated car-door assembly cell with three FANUC R-2000iB/165F robots, two conveyor lines, and pick-and-place and welding operations. Built RSLogix 5000 ladder logic and a FactoryTalk HMI for remote production control and bidirectional PLC–robot communication, enabling validation before physical testing.
